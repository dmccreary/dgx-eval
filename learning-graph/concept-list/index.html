    Concept List¶

    The numbered concepts below are the nodes of the learning graph for DGX Evaluation for Textbook Generation. Each number is the ConceptID used in learning-graph.csv. Most sections follow the 16 topics in the course description. Three sections hold concepts taken from the book's own pages: Prerequisite Skills, Candidate Models Under Evaluation, and Testing Architecture and Containment.

    The course is DGX-model-agnostic, so the hardware concepts list both branches of each variation (CPU architecture and memory layout) and not the specifications of any one DGX model.
