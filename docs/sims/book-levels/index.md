## About This MicroSim

Intelligent textbooks are grouped into five levels. Each level adds a capability to the one below it: interactive content, then adaptation to the reader, then a conversational tutor, then autonomous generation.

This MicroSim is reused from the ibook-skills catalog. It draws the levels as a staircase. The dashed outline marks the three levels that collect data about the reader and are therefore the most regulated.

## How to Use

- Point at a step to read what that level adds. On a touch screen, tap the step.
- Move up the staircase one step at a time and note what each level has that the one below it lacks.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will compare the five levels of intelligent textbooks by the capability each level adds over the one below it.

### Grade Level

Professional development and adult continuing education

### Duration

5 minutes

### Prerequisites

- No prior knowledge of intelligent textbooks is needed

### Activities

1. **Explore** (3 min): Point at each level in turn, starting at Level 1, and say what it adds before reading the description.
2. **Discuss** (2 min): This book is a Level 2 textbook. Name one feature it would need to reach Level 3 and the reader data that feature would require.

### Assessment

A learner who has understood the levels can name the capability that each level adds over the one below it.
