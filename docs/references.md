# References

Works that report on or build on this project. Each chapter also has its own
annotated references page, linked from the chapter list.

1. [Small Language Models as Textbook Generators: A Local-Hardware Benchmark on the NVIDIA DGX Spark and the Intelligent Textbook That Teaches It](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23607610) - Dan McCreary - Zenodo, 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23607610 - The paper behind this book. It reports a three-stage benchmark (learning graph, chapter structure, chapter text) of ten open-weight models on one 128 GB DGX Spark-class machine, a model-free chapter-conformance score, and a cost model checked against measured run times. All results are single runs with no human review of the chapters and no cloud baseline. This book is the 23-chapter open textbook the paper describes. The archived record is at [zenodo.org/records/23607610](https://zenodo.org/records/23607610).
