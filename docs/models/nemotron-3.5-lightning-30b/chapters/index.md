# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 12 chapters covering 199 concepts from the Algebra I course.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Foundations](01-foundations/index.md) - Algebraic expressions, order of operations, variable identification, coefficients, constants, and function concept
2. [Linear Equations](02-linear-equations/index.md) - Solving one-step through multi-step equations, literal equations, inequalities, and graphing
3. [Functions and Notation](03-functions-and-notation/index.md) - Function concept, notation, domain, range, and function operations
4. [Graphing and Coordinates](04-graphing-and-coordinates/index.md) - Coordinate plane, intercepts, slope, and graphing lines
5. [Polynomials and Factoring](05-polynomials-and-factoring/index.md) - GCF, trinomials, difference of squares, polynomial operations
6. [Quadratic Functions](06-quadratic-functions/index.md) - Solving quadratics, quadratic formula, completing the square, and parabola properties
7. [Exponents and Radicals](07-exponents-and-radicals/index.md) - Laws of exponents, rational exponents, radical expressions, and exponential models
8. [Systems of Equations](08-systems-of-equations/index.md) - Solving systems by graphing, substitution, elimination, and solution types
9. [Statistics and Data Analysis](09-statistics-and-data-analysis/index.md) - Scatterplots, correlation, regression, data displays, and statistical questions
10. [Sequences and Series](10-sequences-and-series/index.md) - Arithmetic and geometric sequences, recursive and explicit rules
11. [Mathematical Modeling](11-mathematical-modeling/index.md) - Exponential/quadratic models, linear models, and real-world applications
12. [Advanced Topics and Review](12-advanced-topics-and-review/index.md) - Complex numbers, remaining concepts, and review

## How to Use This Textbook

This textbook is designed to be studied sequentially, as each chapter builds on concepts from previous chapters. Dependencies between concepts have been respected in the chapter design, so students should complete chapters in order for the best learning progression.

Make sure to complete the prerequisite chapters before moving to advanced chapters. Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered and their Concept Impact Scores, which indicate the relative importance of each concept in the overall course.

---

**Note:** The chapter structure respects the dependency relationships (DAG) from the learning graph. Some concepts in later chapters depend on concepts in earlier chapters—please complete prerequisite chapters first.
