# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 12 chapters covering 200 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Algebra Foundations](01-algebra-foundations/index.md) - Introduction to variables, constants, and basic algebraic expressions.
2. [Exponents and Radicals](02-exponents-radicals/index.md) - Rules of exponents, simplifying radicals, and scientific notation.
3. [Linear Equations](03-linear-equations/index.md) - Solving one-step, multi-step equations and properties of equality.
4. [Linear Inequalities and Absolute Value](04-linear-inequalities-abs-value/index.md) - Solving and graphing linear inequalities and absolute value equations.
5. [Introduction to Functions](05-intro-to-functions/index.md) - Understanding relations, functions, domain, range, and the coordinate plane.
6. [Linear Functions and Slope](06-linear-functions-slope/index.md) - Slope, rate of change, and writing linear equations in various forms.
7. [Systems of Linear Equations](07-systems-linear-equations/index.md) - Solving systems of equations using substitution, elimination, and graphing.
8. [Polynomials and Factoring](08-polynomials-factoring/index.md) - Operations with polynomials and factoring techniques.
9. [Quadratic Functions](09-quadratic-functions/index.md) - Properties of parabolas and solving quadratic equations.
10. [Exponential Functions](10-exponential-functions/index.md) - Exponential growth and decay, and comparing exponential to quadratic models.
11. [Data Analysis and Statistics](11-data-analysis-stats/index.md) - Scatterplots, correlation, and linear regression.
12. [Sequences and Series](12-sequences-series/index.md) - Arithmetic and geometric sequences and their formulas.

## How to Use This Textbook

This textbook is designed to be read sequentially. Each chapter builds upon the concepts introduced in previous chapters, ensuring a logical progression from foundational algebra to advanced functions and data analysis. We recommend completing all exercises in one chapter before moving to the next.

**Note:** Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered. Make sure to complete prerequisites before moving to advanced chapters.
