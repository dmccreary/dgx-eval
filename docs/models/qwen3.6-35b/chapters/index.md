# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 11 chapters covering 200 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Foundational Number Concepts and Properties](01-foundational-concepts-and-properties/index.md) - Introduces number types from natural numbers through real numbers, the real number line, absolute value, and fundamental algebraic properties that underpin all future work.

2. [Variables, Expressions, and Polynomial Foundations](02-variables-expressions-polynomial-foundations/index.md) - Builds the language of algebra — variables, constants, coefficients, terms, algebraic expressions, evaluating expressions, expanding and factoring, polynomial classification, and operations on polynomials.

3. [Facturing Techniques and Polynomial Division](03-factoring-techniques-polynomial-division/index.md) - Covers advanced facturing methods from GCF through trinomial factoring, special products, polynomial division, synthetic division, and the remainder and factor theorems connecting roots to factors.

4. [Linear Equations and Inequalities](04-linear-equations-inequalities/index.md) - Progresses from one-step through multi-step equations, literal equations and formulas, then inequalities including compound inequalities and absolute value equations and inequalities, with three methods for solving systems of equations and systems of inequalities.

5. [Linear Relationships and Coordinate Graphing](05-linear-relationships-coordinate-graphing/index.md) - Introduces the coordinate plane, slope as rate of change, direct variation, distance and midpoint formulas, scatter plots, and all three linear equation forms — slope-intercept, standard, and point-slope.

6. [Quadratic Functions](06-quadratic-functions/index.md) - Covers quadratic expressions through completing the square, parabola properties, vertex form, intercepts, discriminant and nature of solutions, the quadratic formula, and facturing quadratics.

7. [Functions and Relations](07-functions-relations/index.md) - Introduces relations versus functions, domain and range, evaluation, function notation, zeros and intercepts, inverse functions, composition, piecewise functions, and transformations — translating algebraic work into function thinking.

8. [Exponential Functions and Sequences](08-exponential-functions-sequences/index.md) - Covers exponential growth and decay, parent functions, graphing with asymptotes, transforming exponentials, base e, plus arithmetic and geometric sequences with recursive and explicit formulas.

9. [Sequences and Statistics](09-sequences-and-statistics/index.md) - Explores arithmetic and geometric sequences, correlation types, line of best fit, regression analysis, residuals, strength of correlation, data distributions, frequency tables, stem-and-leaf plots, box-and-whisker plots, and measures of central tendency through standard deviation.

10. [Mathematical Modeling and Applications](10-mathematical-modeling-applications/index.md) - Synthesizes course content through real-world modeling — translating word problems, linear modeling, exponential modeling with transformations, growth/decay rates, parent functions for comparison, and mathematical models in STEM contexts.

11. [Radicals and Radical Equations](11-radicals-and-equations/index.md) - Completes the study of radicals — radical notation, converting between radical and exponent forms, simplifying square/cube/nth roots, performing arithmetic operations on radicals, and solving radical equations.

## How to Use This Textbook

Each chapter builds on concepts introduced in earlier chapters, following the prerequisite structure mapped in the learning graph. Students should complete chapters in order to ensure all dependencies are satisfied before encountering more advanced topics. The Concepts Covered table in each chapter lists every concept with its Concept Impact Score (CIS), which indicates how broadly that concept influences subsequent material — higher scores mean the concept is foundational to many others.
