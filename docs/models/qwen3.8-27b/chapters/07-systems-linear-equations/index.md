# Systems of Linear Equations

## Summary

This chapter extends linear equations to two or more unknowns. You will solve systems by graphing, substitution, and elimination, and interpret no-solution and infinite-solution cases, then move to systems of inequalities and their feasible regions. By the end, you can choose among solution methods, verify results, and apply systems to realistic word problems.
