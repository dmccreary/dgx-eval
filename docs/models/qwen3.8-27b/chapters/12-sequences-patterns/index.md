# Sequences and Patterns

## Summary

This chapter connects pattern recognition to the functions you have already studied. You will explore arithmetic and geometric sequences, their common difference and common ratio, and explicit and recursive formulas, and determine the nth term. You will see how sequences relate to linear and exponential growth, tying together the ideas of slope, rate, and repeated multiplication.
