# Quadratic Functions and Equations

## Summary

This chapter brings together functions, equations, and graphing in the quadratic. You will study the parabola, its vertex and axis of symmetry, and standard form, then solve quadratics by factoring, square roots, completing the square, and the quadratic formula and interpret the discriminant. You will also model and optimize real-world situations with quadratics, applying the full range of solution methods.
