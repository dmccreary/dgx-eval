# Exponents and Radicals

## Summary

This chapter extends your number skills into powers and roots. You will master exponent notation and the laws of exponents, then move to zero, negative, and rational exponents and build fluency with square and cube roots, radical expressions, and simplifying radicals. By the end, you can simplify exponential and radical expressions and use scientific notation, skills that support the polynomial, quadratic, and exponential work later in the course.
