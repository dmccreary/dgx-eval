# Algebraic Expressions and Variables

## Summary

This chapter makes the jump from arithmetic to algebra by introducing variables, constants, coefficients, and terms. You will learn to read and write algebraic expressions, translate word phrases into algebra, and evaluate and simplify expressions by combining like terms and expanding. After completing it, you can model real-world situations with expressions and move confidently among written phrases, symbolic forms, and evaluated values.
