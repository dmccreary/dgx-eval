# Linear Equations and Inequalities

## Summary

This chapter establishes the equation as the central tool of algebra. Building on the properties of equality, you will solve one-, two-, and multi-step equations, handle equations with variables on both sides, and work with literal equations and the special cases of no or infinitely many solutions. You will then extend these ideas to linear, compound, and absolute value inequalities, and graph and verify your solutions.
