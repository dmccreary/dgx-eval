# Exponential Functions and Models

## Summary

This chapter explores functions that grow and decay by a constant factor. You will define exponential growth and decay, use the growth factor, and graph exponential functions, then apply them to compound interest, percent change, and comparing exponential with linear growth. By the end, you can choose and build exponential models and interpret them in context.
