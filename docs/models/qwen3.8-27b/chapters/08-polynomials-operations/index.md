# Polynomials and Polynomial Operations

## Summary

This chapter broadens the family of expressions from linear to polynomial. You will work with monomials, binomials, and trinomials, identify degree, and add, subtract, and multiply polynomials, including special products. You will also connect polynomials to functions, building the foundation that factoring and quadratics depend on.
