# Statistics and Data Analysis

## Summary

This chapter turns to making sense of data. You will display bivariate data in scatterplots, measure correlation, and fit and interpret a line of best fit, then move to linear regression, two-way frequency tables, and comparing data sets. You will also distinguish correlation from causation and use regressions to make and evaluate predictions.
