# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 14 chapters covering 200 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Number System Foundations](01-number-system-foundations/index.md) - Lays the arithmetic and number-system groundwork for algebra, covering the real number line, absolute value, order of operations, and the commutative, associative, and distributive properties.
2. [Algebraic Expressions and Variables](02-algebraic-expressions/index.md) - Introduces variables, constants, coefficients, and terms, then builds fluency in writing, evaluating, combining like terms, simplifying, and expanding algebraic expressions.
3. [Exponents and Radicals](03-exponents-radicals/index.md) - Develops exponent notation and the laws of exponents, extends to zero, negative, and rational exponents, and builds skill with roots, radicals, and scientific notation.
4. [Linear Equations and Inequalities](04-linear-equations-inequalities/index.md) - Builds the equation as algebra's central tool, progressing from one- to multi-step equations and literal equations, then solving and graphing linear, compound, and absolute value inequalities.
5. [Functions and Relations](05-functions-relations/index.md) - Establishes the function concept through relations, domain, range, and notation across graphs, tables, and mapping diagrams, extending to piecewise and step functions.
6. [Linear Relationships and Slope](06-linear-relationships-slope/index.md) - Develops slope and rate of change, the three forms of a linear equation, parallel and perpendicular lines, and interpreting slope and intercepts in linear models.
7. [Systems of Linear Equations](07-systems-linear-equations/index.md) - Extends linear equations into multiple unknowns, solving systems by graphing, substitution, and elimination, and applying them to systems of inequalities and word problems.
8. [Polynomials and Polynomial Operations](08-polynomials-operations/index.md) - Covers monomials, binomials, and trinomials, their degree, and adding, subtracting, and multiplying polynomials including special products and polynomial functions.
9. [Factoring Polynomials](09-factoring-polynomials/index.md) - Develops systematic factoring, including greatest common factor, trinomials, difference of squares, cubes, and grouping, culminating in fully factored form and a reliable factoring strategy.
10. [Quadratic Functions and Equations](10-quadratic-functions-equations/index.md) - Explores the parabola, vertex, and axis of symmetry, and all four methods for solving quadratics, plus the discriminant and real-world quadratic models.
11. [Exponential Functions and Models](11-exponential-functions-models/index.md) - Develops exponential growth and decay, growth factors, and graphing, applying them to compound interest, percent change, and comparing exponential versus linear growth.
12. [Sequences and Patterns](12-sequences-patterns/index.md) - Studies arithmetic and geometric sequences, common difference and ratio, explicit and recursive formulas, and the nth term, connecting patterns to linear and exponential growth.
13. [Statistics and Data Analysis](13-statistics-data-analysis/index.md) - Analyzes bivariate data with scatterplots, correlation, and line of best fit, extending to linear regression, two-way tables, and distinguishing correlation from causation.
14. [Applied Modeling and Capstone](14-applied-modeling-capstone/index.md) - Integrates the course by building and choosing models, solving multi-step problems, communicating mathematical reasoning, and applying everything in capstone projects.

## How to Use This Textbook

Work through the chapters in order: each chapter builds on the ones before it, and the course's dependency graph is respected throughout, so every concept is introduced only after the ideas it depends on. Foundational topics in the early chapters, such as the number system, expressions, and the function concept, are used repeatedly in the later chapters on linear, quadratic, and exponential models.
