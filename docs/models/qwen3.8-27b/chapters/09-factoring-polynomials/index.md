# Factoring Polynomials

## Summary

This chapter develops factoring as the reverse of multiplication. You will factor out a greatest common factor, factor trinomials, and recognize the difference of squares, perfect square trinomials, sums and differences of cubes, and grouping. You will build a reliable factoring strategy, confirm results by expanding, and express polynomials in fully factored form, the key skill for solving quadratics.
