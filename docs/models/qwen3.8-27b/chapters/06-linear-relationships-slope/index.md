# Linear Relationships and Slope

## Summary

This chapter grounds linear ideas in the coordinate plane. You will plot points, identify intercepts, and develop slope and rate of change, then write linear equations in slope-intercept, point-slope, and standard form. You will graph linear equations, recognize parallel and perpendicular lines, and interpret slope and intercepts in real-world linear models, connecting the algebra to the geometry of a line.
